Privacy Policy

COV Coop mobile app — last updated 16 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information the COV Coop mobile app collects, why we collect it, and what choices you have. The app is provided by COV Cooperative Society for the use of its registered members.

Who we are

COV Cooperative Society ("the cooperative", "we") operates the COV Coop app and is the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Name, member ID, email address, phone number, home address To identify you as a member and to contact you about your account
Profile photo (optional) Shown only to you inside the app
Next-of-kin and emergency contact details Required by the cooperative's membership rules
Bank account details To pay dividends and loan disbursements to you
Financial records — contributions, loans, repayments, dividends To operate your cooperative account and meet statutory audit duties
Approximate location, only while you are checking in to an event To confirm you are at the event venue. Location is not collected in the background and not collected at any other time.
Device push notification token To send you notifications about your account and cooperative events

How we use your information

We use your information only to run your cooperative membership: maintaining your account, processing contributions and loans, paying dividends, confirming event attendance, and contacting you. We do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with other members. We share it only with:

  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Paystack — when you make a payment in the app. Your card details are entered directly into Paystack's own secure checkout and are never seen or stored by the cooperative.
  • Regulators, auditors, or law enforcement where we are legally required to do so.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal details for as long as you are a member. Cooperative financial records are retained after membership ends because we are required by law to keep them for audit.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently erases your name, email address, phone number, home address, profile photo, next-of-kin details, and your push notification registration, and immediately revokes your access.

Your contribution, loan and dividend records are retained in anonymised form, because the cooperative is legally required to keep them for audit. The app tells you this before you confirm.

If you would prefer us to action the deletion for you, email PLACEHOLDER_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Security

Your connection to the app is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes and are never readable by us. You can enable Face ID or fingerprint unlock in the app's settings.

Children

The app is for registered cooperative members and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
