Common questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are for registered members of the cooperative. On the sign-up screen, search for your name, confirm your email address, and enter the one-time code we send you. If your name is not found, contact the cooperative office.
I've forgotten my password.
Tap Forgot Password on the login screen and follow the emailed instructions.
My balance looks wrong.
The cooperative's own books are the authoritative record. Raise it with us through Complaints in the app, or contact the office directly.
How do I make a payment?
Open Deposit from the home screen, enter the amount, and complete the payment through the secure Paystack checkout. Your payment is applied to your cooperative account once confirmed.
How do I check in to an event?
Open Events, select the event, and tap check in while you are at the venue. The app uses your location once, at that moment, to confirm you are there.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently erases your personal details and revokes your access. Cooperative financial records are retained in anonymised form as required by law. Please settle any outstanding loan or contribution first.
I'm not receiving notifications.
Check that notifications are enabled for COV Coop in your device settings, and that Push Notifications is switched on in the app's Settings screen.
