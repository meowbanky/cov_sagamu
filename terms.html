1. Who can use the app

The COV Coop app is provided by COV Cooperative Society for the exclusive use of its registered members. Accounts are created for existing members only; the app does not offer open public registration.

2. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity carried out under your account. Tell us immediately if you believe someone else has gained access to it. Do not share your account with anyone.

3. Acceptable use

You agree not to attempt to access other members' information, interfere with the operation of the app, or use it for any unlawful purpose.

4. Payments

Payments made in the app are for cooperative contributions, loan repayments and related real-world financial services provided by the cooperative outside the app. Payments are processed by Paystack; your card details are entered into Paystack's own secure checkout and are never seen or stored by the cooperative. Payments are applied to your cooperative account once confirmed.

5. Accuracy of information

Balances, loan positions and dividend figures shown in the app are provided for your convenience. The cooperative's own books remain the authoritative record. If a figure looks wrong, contact the cooperative office.

6. Closing your account

You can close your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Closing your account does not cancel any outstanding loan or unpaid contribution obligation to the cooperative, and cooperative financial records are retained as required by law. Please contact the cooperative office before closing your account if you have an outstanding balance.
